2. How Workout Pledge Works — Commitment Contracts

Workout Pledge is a voluntary commitment-contract platform. You set a personal fitness goal, choose a weekly workout target, and pledge a per-miss penalty amount. This is not gambling. The outcome depends entirely on your own behavior — not on chance or on the actions of others — and you are in full control of whether you meet your commitment.

How a pledge works

(a) Create a Pledge. You choose the number of days per week you commit to exercising, a per-miss penalty amount, and attach a valid payment method. Each committed day requires a minimum of 30 minutes of total workout time. Multiple workouts in a single day count — their durations are added together.

(b) Weekly Tracking. All workouts must be recorded on a device that syncs into Apple Health. Your workouts are verified through Apple HealthKit data synced from your device. Each pledge week runs Monday through Sunday in your local time zone (fixed at the time you create the pledge).

(c) Settlement. At the end of each week, the Service evaluates how many of your committed days met the 30-minute requirement. For each committed day where you did not reach the minimum, you are charged your per-miss penalty. Settlement occurs in two phases:

  1. An initial automated evaluation on Monday, opening a 3-day dispute window.
  2. Final settlement the following Sunday night, after the dispute window has closed.

(d) Points Distribution. A portion of penalties collected from users who missed their commitments is converted to points and distributed to users who met their goals, weighted by the number of days pledged. Workout Pledge retains a service fee from penalties to operate and maintain the platform.

(e) Ongoing. Pledges are rolling and continue week to week until you pause or cancel them.

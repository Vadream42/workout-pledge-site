These Official Rules govern the Workout Pledge rewards program (the "Program"). The same rules are bundled inside the Workout Pledge app and are available to users at all times. By participating in the Program, you agree to these Rules.

1. Sponsor

The Workout Pledge rewards program (the "Program") is operated and sponsored by Workout Pledge LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Workout Pledge," "we," "us," or "our"). Contact: info@workoutpledge.app.

2. Apple Is Not a Sponsor

Apple Inc. is NOT a sponsor of, and is NOT involved in any way with, Workout Pledge or this Program. Apple does not administer, endorse, fund, or take any responsibility for the Program, the pledges, the penalties, or the rewards. These Official Rules are an agreement solely between you and Workout Pledge LLC. Any questions, comments, or complaints about the Program must be directed to Workout Pledge LLC, not to Apple.

3. This Is a Skill-Based Program — Not Gambling

Workout Pledge is a voluntary fitness commitment program. Rewards are earned solely through your own verified exercise — by meeting the personal workout goal you set for yourself. The outcome depends entirely on your own effort, not on chance, luck, a drawing, or the actions of other people. This is not a lottery, raffle, sweepstakes, or game of chance. Paying a penalty does not earn you rewards and does not improve your chances of earning them; only completing your committed workouts does.

4. Eligibility

The Program is open only to individuals who are at least 18 years old and who are legal residents of a country where Workout Pledge is available (currently the United States, Canada, and Australia). The Program is void where prohibited or restricted by law. You are responsible for complying with the laws of your own jurisdiction, and you may not participate if doing so is prohibited where you live.

5. Program Period

The Program runs in weekly cycles. Each pledge week begins on Monday and ends the following Sunday, in the local time zone fixed when you create your pledge. Pledges are rolling and continue from week to week until you pause or cancel them. Workout Pledge may modify or end the Program at any time as described in "Changes to These Rules" below.

6. How to Participate

To take part, create an account, confirm you are 18 or older, accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and create a pledge. When you create a pledge you choose (a) the number of days per week you commit to working out (1–7) and (b) a penalty amount for each committed day you miss ($10–$100, in your local currency), and you attach a valid payment method. Each committed day requires at least 30 minutes of total workout time; multiple workouts in the same day are added together toward that 30 minutes.

7. How Rewards Are Determined

At the end of each week, Workout Pledge counts how many of your committed days reached the 30-minute minimum, verified through Apple HealthKit. If you meet your committed number of days, you are a "winner" for that week and share in that week's reward pool. If you miss one or more committed days, you are charged your chosen penalty for each missed day. Winners are determined objectively by verified workout data — never by chance, ranking, or a draw.

8. The Reward Pool and How It Is Funded

Penalties collected from participants who miss their committed days fund the weekly reward pool. Of the penalties collected each week, 50% is retained by Workout Pledge LLC as a platform service fee to operate and maintain the Program, and 50% is converted into points and distributed among that week's winners. The pool is divided among winners in proportion to the number of days each winner pledged. Because the pool depends on how many participants missed their goals in a given week, the number of points a winner receives varies from week to week and may be zero in a week where no penalties are collected.

9. Penalties

By creating a pledge and attaching a payment method, you authorize Workout Pledge to charge your payment method for penalties you incur. A penalty equals the number of committed days you missed multiplied by your chosen per-miss amount. Charges are processed only after the 3-day dispute window for that week has closed (approximately one week after the pledge week ends). All payments are processed by Stripe; Workout Pledge does not store your full card number.

10. Rewards and Redemption

Rewards are issued as points, where 1 point is worth 1 cent (US$0.01 or the local-currency equivalent). Points have no cash value, cannot be transferred or sold, and may be redeemed only within the app for:

  • Amazon gift cards in fixed tiers — 500 points ($5), 1,000 points ($10), 2,500 points ($25), and 5,000 points ($50); or
  • Donations to participating nonprofit organizations, with a minimum redemption of 500 points.

Gift cards are purchased and emailed by Workout Pledge; we aim to fulfill them within 7 business days. Donations are submitted to partner nonprofits on a regular schedule and are not tax-deductible to you. Workout Pledge may change point values, redemption tiers, and available options with reasonable advance notice.

11. Settlement and Dispute Window

Settlement happens in two stages. First, an automated evaluation runs at the start of the week after your pledge week and opens a 3-day window during which you may dispute your result in the app by submitting a written explanation. Second, after the dispute window closes, final settlement charges any penalties and distributes points to winners. We review disputes in good faith. A dispute decision is final under these Rules but does not affect any non-waivable rights you have under applicable consumer-protection law.

12. Verification and Fair Play

Workouts are verified through Apple HealthKit data synced from your device. You agree not to falsify, manipulate, or spoof workout data. We use Apple App Attest and other fraud-detection measures. If we determine that workout data has been manipulated or that these Rules have been violated, we may void the affected results, forfeit related points, charge applicable penalties, and/or suspend or terminate your account.

13. Odds and Approximate Value

Because rewards are based on your own performance rather than chance, every eligible participant who meets their committed goal shares in that week's reward pool — there is no random "winner." The value of points you may earn depends on the size of the weekly pool and the number and pledge size of the winners that week, and therefore cannot be stated in advance. The maximum redeemable reward tier is a $50 (or local-currency equivalent) Amazon gift card at 5,000 points.

14. Taxes

You are solely responsible for any taxes that may apply to rewards you receive, including gift cards. Workout Pledge does not provide tax advice. Consult a tax professional in your country of residence if you have questions.

15. Release and Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, by participating you release Workout Pledge LLC and its officers, employees, and agents from any liability for losses, damages, or injuries arising from participation in the Program or from receiving, using, or redeeming any reward. Workout Pledge is not a medical, fitness, or financial advisory service; consult appropriate professionals before starting any exercise program. Nothing in these Rules excludes any liability that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

16. Governing Law and Disputes

For participants resident in the United States, these Rules are governed by the laws of the State of California, and disputes are resolved as described in the Workout Pledge Terms of Service. For participants resident in Canada or Australia, these Rules are governed by the laws of your country of residence, and nothing in these Rules limits any non-waivable right to bring a claim in your local courts or before a consumer-protection regulator. These Rules incorporate the Workout Pledge Terms of Service; if there is a conflict about the Program specifically, these Rules control.

17. Privacy

Information you provide in connection with the Program is handled in accordance with the Workout Pledge Privacy Policy, available in the app and at workoutpledge.app.

18. Changes to These Rules

Workout Pledge may update these Official Rules from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. Your continued participation after the effective date of the revised Rules constitutes acceptance of the changes. The current version is always available in the app under Profile → Official Rules.
